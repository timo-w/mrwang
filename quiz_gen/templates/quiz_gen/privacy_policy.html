This Privacy Policy should be read alongside the Terms of Use.

3. What information do I collect?

Depending on how you use QuizGen, I may process the following information.

Information you provide

This may include:

  • text entered into the quiz generator;
  • quiz topics and instructions;
  • uploaded lesson files;
  • generated quiz content;
  • information included within uploaded documents; and
  • information you provide when contacting me.

Technical information

I may also process technical information required to operate and secure QuizGen, such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • date and time of requests;
  • technical logs;
  • error information; and
  • information about how features of QuizGen are used.

I seek to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

4. Information contained in uploaded lesson materials

Uploaded lesson materials may contain personal information.

For example, a document could contain:

  • teacher names;
  • pupil names;
  • examples of pupil work;
  • photographs;
  • comments or feedback;
  • email addresses; or
  • other information relating to identifiable individuals.

You should remove personal information that is not necessary before uploading a file.

QuizGen is not intended to be used as a repository for pupil records or other sensitive educational information.

If personal information is included in an uploaded file, that information may be processed as part of providing the requested Service.

5. Why do I process information?

I may process information for the following purposes.

Providing QuizGen

QuizGen processes submitted information so that QuizGen can:

  • process uploaded files;
  • extract relevant lesson content;
  • generate quizzes and other requested resources;
  • return generated content to you; and
  • maintain the functionality of QuizGen.

Maintaining and securing QuizGen

I may process technical information to:

  • monitor the operation of QuizGen;
  • identify and resolve errors;
  • detect misuse;
  • protect against security threats; and
  • maintain the reliability of QuizGen.

Improving QuizGen

I may analyse information about use of QuizGen to understand how it is being used and identify ways to improve its functionality, reliability and educational usefulness.

This may include analysing:

  • the number of quiz generation requests;
  • broad subject areas;
  • types of resources uploaded;
  • features used;
  • generated content;
  • system performance;
  • errors; and
  • general usage patterns.

Where practical, information used for these purposes will be anonymised or aggregated.

Educational research and evaluation

Anonymised or aggregated information may be used to evaluate QuizGen and investigate its educational usefulness.

This may include practitioner inquiry, educational research, presentations, professional meetings, conferences, reports and publications.

For example, I may report that QuizGen generated a particular number of quizzes during a period or was used across a particular range of subject areas.

I will not intentionally publish information that directly identifies individuals or pupils as part of such statistics.

6. My lawful bases for processing

Under the UK GDPR, I need a lawful basis for processing personal information.

Depending on the specific processing activity, I may rely on the following lawful bases.

Contract

Where processing is necessary to provide QuizGen requested by you, I may rely on the contractual necessity lawful basis.

For example, processing an uploaded lesson file may be necessary to generate the quiz you have requested.

Legitimate interests

I may rely on legitimate interests where processing is necessary for purposes such as:

  • maintaining and securing QuizGen;
  • detecting misuse;
  • troubleshooting technical problems;
  • evaluating and improving QuizGen; and
  • understanding general patterns of Service usage.

Where I rely on legitimate interests, I will consider whether the processing is necessary and whether the interests, rights and freedoms of individuals override my interests.

Legal obligations

I may process information where this is necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

The lawful basis used will depend on the particular processing activity.
